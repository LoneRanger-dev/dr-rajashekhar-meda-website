### Full Business Description (750 chars max — for GBP "About" section)

Dr. Rajasekhar Meda is a Consultant Laparoscopic, Endoscopic & Laser Surgeon at Suraksha Hospital, Khammam, with over 10 years of surgical experience. He specialises in advanced minimally invasive keyhole surgery including laparoscopic hernia repair, appendectomy, cholecystectomy (gallbladder removal), colectomy, splenectomy, pancreatectomy, bariatric surgery, and varicose vein laser treatment (EVLA).

Suraksha Hospital provides 24/7 emergency and critical surgical care at Priyadarshini College Old Building, near Karnataka Bank, Nehru Nagar, Wyra Road, Khammam, Telangana. The hospital is equipped with advanced ICU, modern operating theatres, and a multi-specialty team offering expert care, advanced technology, and compassionate healing.
