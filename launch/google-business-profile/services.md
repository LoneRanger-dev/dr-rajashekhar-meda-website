## Surgical Services

### Laparoscopic Surgery
**Description:** Minimally invasive keyhole surgery with small incisions, less pain, faster recovery, and minimal scarring.

### Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy (Gallbladder Removal)
**Description:** Keyhole removal of gallstones and inflamed gallbladder. Most patients are discharged within 24 hours.

### Laparoscopic Appendectomy (Appendix Removal)
**Description:** Emergency and elective keyhole removal of the appendix for acute and chronic appendicitis.

### Laparoscopic Hernia Repair
**Description:** 3D mesh tension-free keyhole repair of inguinal, umbilical, incisional, and ventral hernias.

### Laparoscopic Colectomy
**Description:** Keyhole removal of diseased segments of the colon for colorectal conditions.

### Laparoscopic Splenectomy
**Description:** Minimally invasive spleen removal for haematological and traumatic conditions.

### Laparoscopic Pancreatectomy
**Description:** Advanced keyhole surgical procedure for pancreatic disease.

### Bariatric Surgery
**Description:** Laparoscopic weight loss surgery for obesity management and metabolic conditions.

## Laser Surgery

### Varicose Vein Laser Treatment (EVLA)
**Description:** Endovenous laser ablation for painful, twisted varicose veins — no large incisions, immediate mobility.

### Laser Surgery for Piles / Fissure / Fistula
**Description:** Minimally invasive laser proctology for haemorrhoids, anal fissures, and fistulas.

### Tumor & Cyst Excision
**Description:** Precise surgical removal of benign and malignant tumors, lipomas, cysts, and soft tissue swellings.
