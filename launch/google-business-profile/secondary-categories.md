# Google Business Profile — Secondary Categories

Add these in order of priority under Edit Profile → Business category (up to 9 additional):

1. **Laparoscopic Surgeon** — highest relevance for keyhole surgery queries
2. **General Surgeon** — broad general surgery searches
3. **Hospital** — covers Suraksha Hospital as the parent entity
4. **Medical Clinic** — multi-specialty clinic queries
5. **Emergency Care Physician** — 24/7 emergency positioning
6. **Gastroenterologist** — gastro entrology/gastro surgery queries (Image 1 confirms Gastro Entrology service)

---

### How to add secondary categories
1. business.google.com → Edit Profile → Business category
2. After selecting primary, click **+ Add another category**
3. Search and add each category above in order

> GBP allows up to 10 total categories (1 primary + 9 secondary).
